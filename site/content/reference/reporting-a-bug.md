# Reporting a bug

Open an issue on the [repository](https://github.com/owenb321/MiSTer_h264_decoder/issues) and include:

- the **core version** from the OSD's About line (`H264 <version> <yymmdd>`);
- the **program** that was feeding the core, and its version;
- what the stream was (resolution, frame rate, bitrate, the audio codec, if known);
- which output (HDMI or analog) and what you saw; a photo helps.

The issue forms ask for exactly these. Never attach copyrighted media.
